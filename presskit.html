Bio

Sasha Marina Ruiz de Aguirre (Versasha) is a DJ and producer from Guadalajara, Mexico. Her DJ sets focus on alternative music genres such as Hyperpop, Darkwave and Chiptune. Her music production takes Chiptune and Industrial elements to create noisy soundscapes.

Sasha also founded Cyberia Collective, a group of DJs that esapes the regular House and Minimal Techno trend in Mexico.
